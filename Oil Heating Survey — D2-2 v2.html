2. D2-2 / DISTO Plan

Recommended site workflow: pair the D2-2 to the Leica DISTO Plan app, use Smart Room, measure the room clockwise or anticlockwise, then export the plan/report. This calculator accepts a simple pasted measurement list and also keeps a manual D2-2 fallback.

For a robust import, paste lines such as:
Room: Living Room; Length: 5.12; Width: 4.03; Height: 2.40

5. Radiator sizing

Indicative EN 442-style ΔT correction using a typical exponent of about 1.3. Panel outputs are generic planning values, not manufacturer ratings. Final radiator selection should use the selected manufacturer's published output at the chosen flow/return condition.
